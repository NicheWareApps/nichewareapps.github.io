Privacy Policy

CarPlateBlurrer (Android, package com.nicheware.autoblur)
Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short

  • Your photos and videos are processed on your phone. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics. We do not collect any personal data.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device data for ads, as described below. In the EU/UK you are asked for consent first.

1. Who we are

CarPlateBlurrer is developed by NicheWare (“we”), based in Lithuania, European Union. Contact: nichesoftwaredeve@gmail.com.

2. Photos and videos you choose

When you pick a photo or video, the app reads it through Android’s own photo picker. The app only gets access to the item you select; it does not ask for permission to read your whole gallery.

Finding and hiding license plates happens entirely on your device, using a detection model built into the app. Your photos and videos, and the blurred results, are never sent to us or to anyone else.

The blurred result is saved on your device, in Pictures/PlateBlur or Movies/PlateBlur, or in a folder you choose in Settings. While working, the app keeps a temporary copy of the last processed file in its private storage on your device, which other apps cannot access. It is replaced by the next file you process and deleted when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

3. Settings

Your settings (blur style, detection sensitivity, save folder) are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). This is the only reason the app uses the internet.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process, for example:

  • your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, clicks), and diagnostic data about the ads SDK.

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google’s use of this data is described in the How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services page and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices about ads

  • EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the app, Google’s consent form asks whether you agree to personalised ads and related data use. You can change your choice at any time in the app: Settings → Privacy & ads → Ad privacy choices. If you do not consent, you may see limited or non-personalised ads.
  • All users: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone’s settings (usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The legal basis for personalised advertising in the EU/EEA and UK is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time as described above.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not run our own servers and do not collect analytics or crash reports ourselves.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share your photos or videos with anyone.

6. Children

The app is intended for people selling cars and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly process data of children.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, we usually have nothing to give or delete — your photos, videos and settings stay on your device, where you control them. For data collected by Google for advertising, you can use your ad settings described above or contact Google.

If you have questions, contact us at nichesoftwaredeve@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Lithuania this is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (Valstybinė duomenų apsaugos inspekcija).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
